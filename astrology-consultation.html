Parashari System

The most widely used system of Vedic astrology, named after Sage Parashara. This system forms the foundation of most Hindu astrology practiced today.

We analyze planetary positions in 12 houses, 27 nakshatras, and calculate various dashas (planetary periods) including Vimshottari Dasha (120-year cycle). This system provides comprehensive life predictions including career, relationships, health, and spiritual growth.

Special techniques used: Ashtakavarga, Shadbala, Vargas (Divisional charts), and Transit analysis (Gochara).

Vedic Jyotish

The traditional science of light that encompasses both predictive astrology and spiritual guidance. Jyotish means "light of the divine."

Beyond mere prediction, this system focuses on understanding karmic patterns and providing remedies to improve one's destiny. It incorporates spiritual practices, mantras, yantras, and gemstone therapy to harmonize planetary influences.

Special features: Karmic analysis, past life indications, spiritual evolution guidance, and integration with Vedic rituals and practices.

Nadi Astrology

An ancient system where predictions are based on palm leaf manuscripts written by sages thousands of years ago. These leaves contain records of individuals' lives.

We use principles from Nadi astrology to provide remarkably specific predictions about past, present, and future events. This system is especially powerful for identifying past life karmas and their present effects.

Key applications: Identifying root causes of current problems, precise event timing, relationship karmas, and specific remedial measures from ancient texts.

Kerala Tradition

The unique astrological tradition from Kerala known for its precision in Muhurta (auspicious timing) and Prashna (horary astrology).

This system excels in answering specific questions and selecting perfect timing for important events. It incorporates unique techniques like KP System (Krishnamurti Paddhati) for precise predictions.

Specialties: Accurate answer to specific questions, pinpoint timing for events, marriage matching, and business success timing.
