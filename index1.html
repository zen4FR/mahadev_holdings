📿 Pousha Month

Pousha Month – The month of holy penance, religious rituals, and spiritual purification

Pousha is one of the most sacred months in the Nepali Hindu calendar, a month deeply connected to penance, religious rituals, spiritual purification, and divine grace. This month honors the blessings of Lord Surya, Goddess Lakshmi, and the spiritual light of winter religious festivals. The pujas and rituals performed in the Pousha month are considered especially fruitful.

PUJAS & RITUALS

Birth Rituals (Nwaran and Pasni)

Nwaran is the sacred naming ceremony performed to bless the newborn with a prosperous life. Pasni, the first rice-feeding ceremony, marks the child's growth and introduces them to solid food. These rituals are performed for the child's well-being, protection, and longevity.

Puja Details Book Now

Marriage Rituals

Marriage is one of the most important rituals in Hindu culture. Marriage rituals invoke Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi to pray for harmony, prosperity, and lifelong companionship for the couple. This puja strengthens the sacred bond and blesses the beginning of a new household.

Shraddha Puja

Shraddha is the sacred ritual performed to honor and remember our departed loved ones. This puja brings peace to the souls of ancestors and bestows blessings upon the entire family. It is a deeply respectful ceremony performed with devotion and gratitude.
